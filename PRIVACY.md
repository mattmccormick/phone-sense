# Privacy Policy

Screen Budget collects and stores screen-time totals, app usage, goals, and settings only on your device. No data leaves your device through Screen Budget. The app has no `INTERNET` permission, no account system, no analytics, and no advertising.

Android usage access lets Screen Budget read which apps were used and for how long. This information is used only to calculate the totals and budgets shown in the app. Screen Budget does not read the content of other apps.

If you choose to export your data, Android asks you where to save the file. Screen Budget does not upload exported files or send them to anyone.

You can delete Screen Budget's stored data by clearing its storage or uninstalling the app.
