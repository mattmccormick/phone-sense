# Privacy Policy

Phone Sense collects and stores screen-time totals, app usage, goals, and settings only on your device. No data leaves your device through Phone Sense. The app has no `INTERNET` permission, no account system, no analytics, and no advertising.

Android usage access lets Phone Sense read which apps were used and for how long. This information is used only to calculate the totals and budgets shown in the app. Phone Sense does not read the content of other apps.

If you choose to export your data, Android asks you where to save the file. Phone Sense does not upload exported files or send them to anyone.

You can delete Phone Sense's stored data by clearing its storage or uninstalling the app.
