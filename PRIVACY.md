# Privacy Policy

Phone Sense processes screen-time totals, app usage, goals, and settings on your device. It has no `INTERNET` permission and does not send data to its own or a third-party server. It has no account system, analytics, or advertising.

Android backup is enabled for Phone Sense. If device backup is enabled, Android may copy the app's usage database and settings to the backup account configured on your device. Android may also transfer that app data directly to another device when you use device-to-device transfer. These backups and transfers are handled by Android and the backup provider, not by Phone Sense; their availability and handling depend on your device's backup settings and provider. You can manage or turn off cloud backup in Android's **Settings → Google → Backup**. Device-to-device transfer is controlled during the transfer process.

Phone Sense stores its data in Android's private app storage and relies on Android's app sandbox to restrict access by other apps. Phone Sense does not operate a server that receives your data.

Android usage access lets Phone Sense read which apps were used and for how long. This information is used only to calculate the totals and budgets shown in the app. Phone Sense does not read the content of other apps.

If you choose to export your data, Android asks you where to save the file. Phone Sense writes the export to the destination you choose and does not upload it or send it to anyone. The destination and any backup service for it are subject to their own settings and terms.

You can delete Phone Sense's on-device data by clearing its storage or uninstalling the app. This does not necessarily remove a copy already saved by Android backup or an export destination; manage those copies through the relevant backup settings or service.
