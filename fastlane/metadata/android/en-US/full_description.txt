Phone Sense helps you understand your phone habits and make deliberate choices about how you spend your time. Reduce your usage at your own pace; Phone Sense does not block apps. Set a weekly screen-time goal and see how much time you can use today while still meeting it. A six-week chart shows your progress over time, and home-screen widgets keep today's budget and the chart in view.

Review each day's activity by app, exclude apps that are not distractions, and enter missed days by hand. You can export a backup or spreadsheet and restore a backup later. Phone Sense processes data on your device and does not send it to a server. If Android device backup is enabled, Android may back up the app's usage database and settings to your configured backup account or transfer them directly to another device. Manage cloud backup in Android settings.

Phone Sense can send four kinds of notification:

* A daily update showing how much screen time remains in today's budget
* A weekly summary with your average, whether you met your goal, and a suggested goal you can accept
* A reminder when the current week does not have a goal
* An alert if usage access is turned off and screen time cannot be collected

Notifications are optional. You choose their time and can turn them off in Android settings.

To measure screen time, Phone Sense asks for Android's usage access permission. This permission lets the app read when other apps were used. Phone Sense uses that information on your device to calculate daily totals. It does not read the content of other apps, and it has no internet permission. Android backup and device transfer are controlled by Android and your device settings.
